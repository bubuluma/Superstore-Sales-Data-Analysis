SUPERSTORE SALES DATA ANALYSIS
Tools: Python, SQL, Microsoft Excel and Power BI
1. Introduction
The purpose of this project was to analyse Superstore sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate product and customer performance, and provide recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.
The analysis was conducted using Python, SQL and Power BI, with Microsoft Excel used as the original data source.
2. Dataset Overview
The dataset contained 9,800 records and 18 columns, covering transactions from January 2015 to December 2018.
The dataset included information about customer segments, product categories, geographical locations, order dates, shipping methods and sales values.
Following data cleaning, 9,794 records were retained for sales analysis.
3. Methodology
The project followed these analytical stages:
Data cleaning: Python and Pandas were used to inspect data types, identify missing values, check duplicates, convert date columns and prepare the dataset for analysis.
Exploratory data analysis: Python, Matplotlib and Seaborn were used to examine sales trends, customer segments, product categories, geographical performance and shipping methods.
SQL analysis: SQL queries were used to calculate total sales, analyse annual performance, identify top-performing products and customers, and compare geographical sales.
Data visualisation: Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard containing key performance indicators, sales trends, product performance and customer analysis.
4. Key Findings
The analysis identified the following findings:
1.	Total recorded sales amounted to approximately $2.26 million.
2.	The year 2018 recorded the highest sales, generating approximately $721,351.
3.	Technology was the highest-performing product category, generating approximately $825,529.
4.	Consumer customers contributed approximately $1.15 million, representing 50.8% of total sales.
5.	The West region generated the highest geographical sales, contributing approximately $710,220.
6.	Phones and Chairs were the highest-revenue product subcategories.
7.	Standard Class was the most significant shipping method by recorded sales.
8.	Sales declined by approximately 4.2% between 2015 and 2016.
5. Recommendations
Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:
•	Strengthen marketing and customer retention strategies targeting Consumer customers.
•	Prioritise inventory management for high-revenue products, particularly Phones and Chairs.
•	Investigate opportunities to expand sales in lower-performing geographical regions.
•	Maintain strategies that support growth in the Technology category.
•	Monitor shipping performance to improve delivery efficiency and customer experience.
•	Investigate historical sales declines and identify opportunities to improve revenue consistency.
6. Conclusion
The Superstore Sales Data Analysis project demonstrated how data analytics tools can be used to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.
The integration of Python, SQL and Power BI enabled the identification of revenue trends, high-performing products, customer segments and geographical markets.
The findings provide management with useful information to support marketing strategies, inventory planning, customer engagement and business growth.
Further analysis incorporating profit, quantity, discount and returns information would provide a more comprehensive understanding of business performance.
